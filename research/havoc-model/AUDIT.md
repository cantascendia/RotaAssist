# Havoc model source audit (Midnight 12.1.0)

This audit compares the **loaded** addon model at `dba4dc328b79468967a158360634ce47b4c4277b` with pinned SimulationCraft commit [`774babd`](https://github.com/simulationcraft/simc/tree/774babde5ddc7c5fc9f1abb129b473f8a076df70), whose generated spell data names game build **12.1.0.69875**. The baseline was exported to [`dist/research/havoc-loaded-model.json`](../../dist/research/havoc-loaded-model.json) (SHA-256 `6E88715928FAB1FE9683F465CFB917FF5E8FAE3CF0149BCB94AF2C45F3E01BBE`). A separate [single-fight full-state trace](../../dist/research/simc/trace/raw-full-1t-30s.json) has SHA-256 `092BE4D730E0A7434CC5AB9BCA822E8F72CF820B8CC569C0601A5BAE38AE6C5A`. It is a structural observation, not a DPS benchmark.

In combat, Fury and cooldown results may be secret; the addon must fail closed rather than infer readiness from a missing value. The small fixes above correct documented constants and remove a non-castable recommendation without claiming full SimC parity.


## Blacklist contract and final validation

The source-confirmed non-castable Glaive Tempest expands the exact blacklist
contract from {203555, 290271, 412713} to {203555, 290271, 412713, 342817}.
The two existing exact-set tests were updated under Test-Lock scenario 1
(`spec-change:`), retaining all three old IDs and checking the new one. New
behavioral tests use scenario 3 (`test:`). Five isolated mutations of generator,
cooldown, cap and passive behavior were all rejected by those tests.

Final same-row replay: each revision/profile maps733 rows, predicts605, and
agrees exactly128 plus179 form-equivalent actions. No same-row choice improvement
was observed. Full counts and matching row sets are in `replay-results.json`.
Astra completed the final code changes and review after the user ended Sol work.
